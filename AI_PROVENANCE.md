# AI development provenance

## Purpose

This file records development provenance for `feregion`. It is separate from the
project software license, from third-party and scientific-source provenance, and from
version-specific verification or scientific validation evidence.

## Scope of AI assistance

The `feregion` 0.x line has been developed with extensive generative-AI assistance.
Most literal implementation has been generated or revised using generative-AI systems,
and tests, documentation, benchmark/development tooling, and release-support material
have also been materially AI-assisted. The project does not claim complete per-line
historical attribution.

## Human role and release authority

The human maintainer defines and directs requirements, architecture, public behavior,
scientific expectations, acceptance criteria, and release decisions; selects or rejects
proposed changes; directs revisions; evaluates applicable testing, review, and validation
evidence; and authorizes releases. This statement does not imply that every generated
line was manually inspected.

## AI tooling

OpenAI generative-AI systems have been materially used in development. Complete
historical model, product, and version identity was not retained consistently, so this
project does not reconstruct or invent historical tooling identities.

## Generation boundary

AI assistance has included source code, tests, documentation, benchmark/developer
tooling, and release-support material. AI generation does not establish correctness,
scientific validity, ownership, originality, or non-infringement.

## Scientific-source boundary

Generative-AI systems are not the authority for Flinn–Engdahl region definitions,
names, hierarchy, identifiers, or source data. Those originate in separately identified
scientific and upstream sources. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and
`src/feregion/data/metadata.json`.

## Third-party material

Dependencies and copied or adapted third-party material retain their own rights. AI
processing does not convert third-party material into original project material. A finite
review cannot establish universal originality or absence of third-party rights.

## Verification and validation

AI generation is not verification. Package correctness and scientific compatibility are
supported only to the scope of version-specific tests, source/reference comparisons,
traceability, benchmark evidence, and reviews. Their limitations remain part of those
records.
